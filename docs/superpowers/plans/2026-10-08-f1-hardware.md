# F1 Hardware Company Implementation Plan
> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans; Native, no subagents. Track checkbox steps.
**Goal:** Research, design, manufacture and profitably sell independent CPU/GPU/motherboard/RAM/storage products.
**Architecture:** Content-driven research and spec constraints; command handlers are immutable and tick hooks own development, inventory and sales. Engine output schema gains defaulted arrays so F0 saves still load.
**Tech Stack:** Existing strict TypeScript, Zod, React, Vitest, Playwright.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-pc-tycoon-enhanced-foundation-design.md
## Global Constraints
- UI非依存の決定論的TypeScriptエンジンでゲームを進行。
- ゲームバランス係数は全て設定データ化し、架空のゲーム数値と実世界の工学指標をUIで区別する。
- 製品カテゴリ別の制約付きスペックフォーム。
- 支出超過は明示的に拒否するか、許可された借入残高として表現する。
## Review Focus
- Unknown/prerequisite-locked research and duplicate ids reject atomically (Task 1).
- Fractional/NaN/out-of-range specs and prices reject (Task 1).
- Developing products cannot launch or manufacture (Task 2).
- Production budget/capacity never allows negative stock/cash (Task 2).
- Expensive unsold products and save/replay preserve deterministic market behavior (Task 2).
